What it does

Made for the long drive

Big things live on country roads, where the signal drops out. The app is built around that.

🗺️

The whole country, at a glance

Pins cluster by area and split apart as you zoom in, each one coloured and marked by what kind of big thing it is.

📍

What's near me?

One tap frames where you are and your five closest big things. Sort the list by distance to see how far each one is.

🏷️

Filter by state, kind and fate

Just Queensland's fauna? Only the ones still standing? Filters apply to the map and the list alike.

♥️

Save your shortlist

Heart the ones you want to see, then show just those on the map. Your saved list stays on your device.

📶

Works without signal

Every big thing ships inside the app, so the map, list and details work offline. Photos you've seen stay cached.

🎯

Honest pins

When a source only names the town, the pin says so: a dashed outline marks the town's centre, not the sculpture.

💀

Gone, but noted

Demolished and removed big things stay on the map, greyed out, so you don't drive two hours to an empty lot.

🌙

Day and night

A warm paper look by day and a dark mode for the evening drive, with text that grows with your Dynamic Type size.

A look inside

Map, list, and the story behind each one

iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo

Room to spread out

On a big screen the list and the details sit side by side, so you can flick through big things without losing your place. On iPhone Duo it fits both displays: the outer screen when it's closed, and the list beside the details when you open it.

Small touches

  • Tap the little map on any big thing to jump to it on the big map.
  • One tap opens it in Apple Maps for directions.
  • See when there's an EV charger nearby.
  • Every photo is credited to the person who took it.

Where it comes from

Open data, checked by hand

Big Things draws on the same dataset as bigthings.downunder.blue, gathered from open sources and corrected where they were wrong. One census counts 1,075 big things nationally; the app maps the 481 that can be found. It checks for updates when you open it, and never overwrites what you have with a bad download.
