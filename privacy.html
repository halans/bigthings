Privacy

Last updated 23 November 2026

Big Things doesn't collect personal data. There's no account, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking code in the app.

Your location

  • The app asks for your location only when you tap “Near me” on the map or sort the list by distance, and only while you're using it.
  • It only needs your position to within about 100 metres, enough to tell which big things are closest.
  • Your location is used on your device to frame the map and work out distances. The app doesn't send it anywhere or store it.
  • You can turn location off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.

Your saved big things

The big things you save are stored on your device only. They aren't uploaded, and deleting the app deletes them.

What the app downloads

  • The app downloads updates to the list of big things, and their photos, from bigthings.downunder.blue. Like any website, that server receives the standard information your device sends with a request, such as its IP address. The app sends nothing else with these requests.
  • Maps are provided by Apple. Apple's privacy policy covers map data.
  • Links to Apple Maps, Wikipedia, Wikidata and photo credits open in those apps or your browser, under their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
