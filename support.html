Support

Need a hand with Big Things, or spotted a big thing that's missing or wrong? Email support@halans.dev and include your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version if something isn't working.

Location and “Near me”

  • The app asks for your location only when you tap “Near me” or sort the list by distance. If nothing happens, check Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Big Things.
  • Your location stays on your device. See the privacy policy for details.

Using it without signal

Every big thing ships inside the app, so the map, list and details work offline. Photos you've already seen stay cached, but photos you haven't opened yet need a connection the first time. Apple Maps tiles are provided by Apple and may be patchy far from the signal.

A pin is in the wrong place

When a source only names the town, the pin is drawn with a dashed outline to show it marks the town's centre, not the sculpture itself. If a solid pin is in the wrong spot, email the name, the town and where it should be.

A big thing is missing, moved or gone

The data comes from open sources and is corrected by hand, and some big things are still missing. Email the name and town, with a link to a source or a photo if you have one. Demolished and removed big things stay on the map, greyed out.

My saved big things disappeared

Saved big things are stored on your device only, so deleting the app deletes them. They aren't backed up by the app.

Which devices work?

Big Things needs iOS 27 or later and runs on iPhone and iPad. There is also a web map that works in any browser.
